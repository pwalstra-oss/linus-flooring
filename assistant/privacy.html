Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Peter Walstra ("we," "us," or the "Brand") collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the personal assistant text-messaging service operated by Peter Walstra. By opting in to receive text messages, you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We collect only the information needed to operate the assistant and communicate with you by text message:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The content of text messages you send to and receive from the assistant.
  • Your name and, where you provide it, the reason for your message (for example, a request, question, or message you want passed along).

How we use your information

We use this information solely to:

  • Send and receive text messages between you and the assistant.
  • Respond to your requests, take messages, and confirm actions before they are taken.
  • Maintain a record of the conversation so the assistant can follow up accurately.

We do not use your information for advertising or marketing, and we do not send promotional messages.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

Service providers

Messages are delivered through our telecommunications provider (Twilio) solely to transmit texts to and from your device. These providers process your information only to deliver the messages and are not permitted to use it for their own marketing.

Your choices

You may stop receiving messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for assistance. You may also contact us using the details below to ask what information we hold or to request its deletion.

Data retention

We keep message records only as long as needed to operate the assistant, and we delete information on request.
